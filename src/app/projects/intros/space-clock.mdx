This object began its life as a General Electric model F1-8147 bedside alarm
clock. When I found it on the shelves of a thrift store, I was struck by the
space age look of its vacuum-fluorescent display and by the boxy 80s silhouette
of it shell. Inspiration struck: this clock was destined for space. I pictured
it at ease on the dashboard of the
[Pan-American Lunar Shuttle](https://youtu.be/0ZoSYsNADtY?t=91), from the film
"2001: A Space Odyssey".

To bring this vision to life, the clock is stripped down to its bare components.
Every button is cleaned and resoldered. The shell is sanded, primed and painted
a lunar shade of white. The iconic NASA logo is carefully applied to both sides,
while the alarm control switch is painted a vibrant red to match. Finally, the
display rim and raised labeling are touched up for contrast.
